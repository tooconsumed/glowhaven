GLOWHAVEN BEAUTY — LOCAL WEBSITE

Pages:
- index.html       Home
- shop.html        Product listing, category filters and price sorting
- product.html     Product detail page (example: product.html?id=2)
- about.html       Brand story
- contact.html     Contact form and FAQ

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the glowhaven-beauty folder.
3. Double-click index.html to preview, OR use a local server:
   - VS Code: install Live Server, right-click index.html, choose "Open with Live Server".
   - Python: open a terminal in this folder and run: python -m http.server 8000
     Then visit http://localhost:8000

Images:
- Product and editorial photography are loaded from images.unsplash.com.
- These are Unsplash-hosted image URLs; the site needs an internet connection to display them.
- Review Unsplash's current license/terms before commercial deployment. Image URLs are remote, not bundled locally.

Functionality:
- Responsive navigation
- Product cards and product detail pages
- Category filtering and price sorting
- Cart with quantity controls and browser localStorage
- Newsletter/contact demo feedback
- Checkout is intentionally a demo and is not connected to payments, inventory, email, or shipping.

Brand name, copy, product names, prices and products are fictional placeholders.
